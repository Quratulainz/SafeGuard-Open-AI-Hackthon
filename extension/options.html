Automatic assistant

On first turn-on, your browser asks for HTTP/HTTPS website access once. You can instead allow just the current website from the popup. Turning this off removes the assistant; it does not scan anything or expand permissions.

Subscription reminders

Reminders start 7 days before renewal or your chosen cancellation-review date. We check at browser startup and hourly, and notify at most once per target date each day. Missed dates are included until you update the record. Long-term subscriptions still receive renewal reminders. Nothing is cancelled automatically.
